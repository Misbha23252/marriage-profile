MISBHA K - MARRIAGE PROPOSAL WEBSITE

SHOWING / HIDING THE EDITING TOOLS (eye icon)
- All editing tools are hidden by default so visitors see a clean website.
- Click the faint green EYE icon (top-left corner) to reveal the editing tools:
  photo Save/Restore buttons, the status text, and the "✎ Edit Text" button.
- Click the eye icon again to hide everything and return to the clean view.

TEXT EDITING (About, Family, Gallery, Values, Contact, Hero)
- With the eye icon ON, click the "✎ Edit Text" button (bottom-right corner).
- Every text item (names, details, headings, address, quote, etc.) becomes
  clickable — click and type to change it.
- Click "Save Changes" to persist all text in the browser.
- Click "Done" to leave edit mode.
- Click "Reset All" to restore the original text.

PHOTO MANAGEMENT (with the eye icon ON)
- There is one main photo frame and four gallery frames.
- Published photos are bundled in photos/ and are visible to every visitor.
- Main: IMG_4160.jpg. Gallery: IMG_4158.jpg, IMG_4159.jpg, IMG_4161.jpg,
  IMG_4160.jpg, in that order.
- Photos of any dimensions fill their frame; edges may be cropped to fit.
- The gallery uses four columns on desktop, two on tablets, and one on small phones.
- With editing tools on, click a photo to select a local replacement.
- Large photos are automatically resized (up to 1600 pixels on the longest side)
  and compressed before saving. Small photos are not enlarged.
- Saved copies use JPEG; transparent areas become white and animations become stills.
- Wait for "Photo ready", then click Save. Originals on your device are unchanged.
- Click Save to persist the replacement ONLY in this browser, not for other visitors.
- Reopen the same website on the same browser/device and the saved photos remain.
- Click Restore published to remove a local replacement and show the public photo.

MOBILE NAVIGATION
- On phones, tap the ☰ menu button (top-right of the nav bar) to open the
  section links (About, Family, Gallery, Values, Contact). Tap a link to jump
  there; the menu closes automatically.

IMPORTANT:
Published photos are public files in this repository and on GitHub Pages.
To change them for EVERY visitor, update the corresponding files in photos/
and commit and push to the publishing branch. Optimize replacement files and
remove metadata before publishing. Previously committed files may remain in
Git history.

The editing tools still use browser local storage. Photo replacements and text
edits made with these tools are local to the device/browser, NOT public updates.
Existing local photo replacements take precedence over the published photos;
use Restore published to see the public version again.
